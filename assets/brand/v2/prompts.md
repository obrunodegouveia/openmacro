# OpenMacro identity II — imagegen prompts

Created with the built-in image_gen tool. The refined drawing was converted into a single-color vector master with Potrace; platform PNGs were exported from that master with Sharp. Wordmark outlines were made from the local Futura Medium typeface with CoreText. The vector files are the production source of truth.

## Concept

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create a new, exceptionally considered identity symbol for OpenMacro, an independent app that teaches people how money and macroeconomic systems actually work. Audience: curious young adults. Personality: cultured, lucid, quietly confident, open. The client rejected a glossy green 3D O with rising bars as generic AI branding.

Art direction: timeless modernist symbol design with contemporary optical refinement. A mark that could be cut from one sheet of paper and remain unmistakable. Flat, one ink color, beautifully weighted positive and negative space, few confident curves, mathematically lucid but optically human. Think the standard of an exceptional international identity studio. Distinctive enough to own; sophisticated enough for an Apple Design Award contender.

Concept: "The open system". Invent a bespoke circular O / lower-case m counterform ligature. An architectural open circle formed from TWO broad opposing curved forms, interlocking by negative space rather than by over-under weaving. A single elegant opening lets the eye enter the form. Economic exchange and opening up knowledge are evoked abstractly. The negative space is an essential part of the logo. Broad flowing surfaces, a surprising but inevitable cut, expertly balanced asymmetry. Do not make a generic broken ring: the geometry must carry a subtle unique OM relationship. Limit the silhouette to two main flat masses; no detached dots.

Color: a SINGLE solid deep pine ink #183D35 on a solid WHITE #FFFFFF background. Absolutely flat ink, perfectly uniform colors.
Composition: ONE centered production logo symbol only, occupying 62% of a square 1024x1024 canvas. Generous white space. No wordmark yet.
Avoid: 3D, bevels, glass, chrome, gradients, texture, drop shadows, glow, ribbon knots, infinity symbols, pinwheels, sparkles, flower/starbursts, rising bar charts, arrows, dollar signs, coins, globes, leaves, generic fintech logos, rounded app tiles, mockups, text, annotation, presentation boards. This must be a sophisticated ORIGINAL logo, not an illustration. Keep shape legible at 24px.
```

## Refinement

The generated concept was the reference image for this edit.

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Refine this OpenMacro OM logo into an immaculate flat identity mark.
Preserve the fundamental two interlocking circular forms and the subtle lowercase m counterform. Improve the drawing: remove needle-like internal cusps, make the two separating cuts clearly open rather than hairline slits, slightly simplify the inner arches so it reads at 24px. Broad precise arcs, deliberate flat terminals, smooth optical curves. Keep the poised asymmetric circular composition.
The final logo must be absolutely ONE FLAT SOLID INK COLOR #183D35. No gradients, no light source, no surface shading, no texture, no 3D whatsoever. This is a flat graphic logo, like crisp vector artwork printed in a single spot ink.
Present the symbol on a FULLY OPAQUE SOLID WHITE #FFFFFF background, including all internal negative spaces, with no alpha and no transparency. One square 1024x1024 image. Logo centered and occupying 62% of canvas. Background extends to all four corners. No text, no surrounding container, no app tile, no mockup. Every shape boundary precise and clean.
```
